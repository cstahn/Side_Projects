# WILDFRAME — Game Concept v0.5

> An adult, original take on the "creature photography" genre.
> Status: early concept / prototype planning.

---

## 1. One-line pitch
You are a wildlife photographer on an uncharted tropical island full of original creatures. You shoot for a magazine, survive in the wild, and quietly gather photo evidence against poachers — one camera, two jobs.

## 2. What makes it "adult"
Not gore or shock. The maturity comes from **responsibility**: your photos have consequences for the animals, the village, and you.

## 3. Core loop
**Gear up in the village → trek into the wild → sneak, survive, shoot → return, edit, sell → upgrade.**

---

## 4. 3D + 2D split
| Mode | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **3D** | The wild | Explore, track creatures, set up hides, take photos, avoid poachers |
| **2D** | Village studio | Edit photos, lay out magazine pages, sort evidence, fill the Field Guide |

### Editing station
- **Default: digital editing** — crop, exposure, color, layouts. Familiar to everyone.
- **Optional Film Mode** — limited rolls (24/36), no preview until you develop them, rarer film = bigger rewards. Film has had a real comeback, so this serves an actual hobby rather than pure nostalgia.

---

## 5. Photography as a simulation
- Real camera controls: shutter, aperture, ISO, focus, lens swaps. **Auto mode** does all of this for new players.
- Creatures run on their own schedules: they eat, hunt, sleep, migrate, react to weather, and learn fear.
- Scoring rewards **behavior** (hunting, feeding young, courtship), composition, and rarity — not just "the animal is in the frame."

## 6. Stealth (both directions)
- Hide from **animals** so they act naturally.
- Hide from **poachers** so they don't catch you.
- Shared tools: camo, wind direction, noise, bait, camera traps, drone, boat.

## 7. Poacher storyline
- Poachers hunt the same animals you track, so the best photo spots are also the most dangerous.
- Evidence must be **readable**: a clear face, a boat number, a trap location. Photo skill = story progress.
- Choices:
  - **Publish** → arrests, but the survivors come after you.
  - **Sell to the company** → money, but creatures vanish from the island.
  - **Stay silent** → things slowly get worse.

## 8. Survival (light)
**Rule: every survival system must create a photo moment or a story moment, or it gets cut.**
- Water and food limit how far a trip can go.
- Shelter doubles as a photo hide (dawn shots from your tent).
- Weather and nights are harsh, but rare creatures come out then.
- Fire keeps you warm, but it scares animals and gives away your position.
- **Relaxed Mode** turns survival off.
- Avoid: fast-draining hunger bars, heavy crafting, losing your photos on death.

---

## 9. The village (home base)
- Safe zone: sleep, save, edit, resupply.
- Shop: food, water, film, batteries, camera gear.
- Trust system: helping the village brings tips and discounts, and angering it closes doors.
- **Someone in the village secretly helps the poachers.** Your photos slowly reveal who.
- The village changes with your choices and can thrive or turn fearful.
- The farther you go from the village, the rarer the creatures and the bigger the risks.

## 10. Tourism businesses (NEW in v0.5)
Each business buys a *different kind* of photo, so players are pushed to shoot in different styles.

| Business | What they want | Gameplay it creates | Possible secret |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tropical Calendar Co.** | 12 beautiful shots, one per "month"/season theme | Landscape and seasonal photography with a strict wishlist | Owner sells location data to the company |
| **Painter** | Reference photos: specific light, colors, poses | Golden-hour and weather hunting | Paintings hide clues about old poaching sites |
| **Story Writer** | Photo *sequences* that tell a story (e.g. a nest from egg to first flight) | Returning to the same spot over days | Is writing an exposé and needs your evidence |
| **Tour Guide** | Knows the safe routes and sells tours | Unlocks shortcuts and hidden areas when trust is high | Could be the poachers' informant, or their biggest enemy |

**More business ideas for later:** a postcard shop, a souvenir carver (wants close-up detail shots), a dive shop (underwater camera), a bird-watching club, a local museum (Field Guide rewards), and a café wall gallery that shows your best shots.

### Why the businesses matter
- **Money:** several buyers, each with its own taste and price.
- **Variety:** stops players from taking the same photo over and over.
- **Story:** every business owner is a suspect, an ally, or both.
- **Tourism vs. nature:** more tourists bring more money *and* more pressure on the animals. The player's work feeds both.

---

## 11. Modern (2026) features
- Weekly online photo contests and a shared gallery.
- A community Field Guide.
- High-res photo export (wallpapers, prints).
- Short sessions for handhelds and long overnight shoots.
- Accessibility: auto camera, Relaxed Mode, full remapping, subtitle and contrast options.

## 12. Build plan
1. **Engine:** Unreal 5 (visual quality) or Godot (small or solo team).
2. **Vertical slice:** 1 small area, 3 creatures, basic camera. Prove that taking photos is fun.
3. Then: editing station → village + 1 business → creature behavior → light survival → poacher story → online features.
